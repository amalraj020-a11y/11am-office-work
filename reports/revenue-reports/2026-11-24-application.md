- **Pricing hypothesis:** Offer a premium add‑on that lets hosts export attendee lists as CSV and remove the branding after a $2.99 one‑time purchase.
- **Free tier:** All core features remain free; premium is optional.
- **Free hosting:** GitHub Pages

## Potential operating costs

- Current prototype: ₹0 within published free‑tier limits

## Potential customers

- local park users
- college dorm residents
- neighborhood association members

## Launch strategy

- Publish repo on GitHub, enable GitHub Pages, share the link in local park community groups and on neighborhood subreddits.
- Gather feedback from 5‑10 users and iterate.
