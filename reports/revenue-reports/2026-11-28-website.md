# Revenue hypothesis — KidSit Scheduler

- **Model:** freemium
- **Pricing hypothesis:** Offer a premium badge for sitters ($2/month) that highlights them on the list; free tier remains fully functional
- **Free tier:** All core features remain free for both parents and sitters
- **Free hosting:** GitHub Pages

## Potential operating costs

- Current prototype: ₹0 within published free-tier limits

## Potential customers

- Parent‑teacher associations
- Community center newsletters
- Neighborhood Facebook groups

## Launch strategy

- Create a simple landing page on GitHub Pages and share the link in local neighborhood social media groups
- Collect feedback from 5 families and 3 sitters to refine UI and validation
