# Revenue hypothesis — StreetSpot Share

- **Model:** freemium
- **Pricing hypothesis:** Charge a small transaction fee (e.g., 5%) on paid reservations once a payment gateway is added, while keeping basic listings free.
- **Free tier:** Unlimited free listings and reservations; premium features like highlighted listings and analytics later.
- **Free hosting:** GitHub Pages

## Potential operating costs

- Current prototype: ₹0 within published free‑tier limits

## Potential customers

- City commuters looking for cheap curbside parking
- Homeowners with unused driveway space
- Small property managers

## Launch strategy

- Publish the repo on GitHub and enable GitHub Pages
- Share the link in local community forums and neighborhood Facebook groups
- Collect feedback and iterate on UI/UX before adding any paid features
