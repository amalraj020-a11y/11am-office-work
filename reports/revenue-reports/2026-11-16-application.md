# Revenue hypothesis — GigTime Tracker

- **Model:** freemium
- **Pricing hypothesis:** Offer a paid ‘Pro’ version that adds PDF invoice generation and multi‑device sync via a tiny paid backend; test conversion at 5% of active users paying $4/month.
- **Free tier:** All core tracking, client management, CSV export, and local storage are free forever.
- **Free hosting:** GitHub Pages

## Potential operating costs

- Current prototype: ₹0 within published free‑tier limits

## Potential customers

- Ride‑share drivers tracking extra gigs
- Graphic designers handling multiple client projects
- Content creators doing short video contracts

## Launch strategy

- Publish repository on GitHub with GitHub Pages enabled
- Share on freelancer forums (r/freelance, r/sidehustle) and social media groups
- Collect feedback via a simple Google Form linked from the app
