- **Pricing hypothesis:** Offer a premium export to PDF and optional cloud backup for $2.99/month after 1,000 active users.
- **Free tier:** All core features remain free; premium adds PDF export and optional encrypted cloud sync.
- **Free hosting:** GitHub Pages

## Potential operating costs

- Current prototype: ₹0 within published free‑tier limits

## Potential customers

- Parents of school‑age children
- College students sharing an apartment
- Co‑living house managers

## Launch strategy

- Publish the site on GitHub Pages and share the link in family‑oriented subreddits and parenting Facebook groups.

- After 50 sign‑ups, add the optional premium export feature.
