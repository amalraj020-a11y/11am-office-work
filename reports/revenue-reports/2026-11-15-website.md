# Revenue hypothesis — BlockParty Board

- **Model:** freemium
- **Pricing hypothesis:** Offer a paid tier that hosts events on a custom domain and provides export to CSV for organizers, starting at $2 per month.
- **Free tier:** Unlimited public events stored locally, no custom domain, no data export.
- **Free hosting:** GitHub Pages

## Potential operating costs

- Current prototype: ₹0 within published free‑tier limits

## Potential customers

- Apartment complex managers
- Local hobby clubs
- Pop‑up market organizers

## Launch strategy

- Publish repository on GitHub with GitHub Pages enabled
- Share the link in local community newsletters and on neighborhood WhatsApp groups
- Collect feedback via a simple Google Form (external, human‑approved) to iterate
