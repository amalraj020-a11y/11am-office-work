# Revenue hypothesis — Local ArtSwap Board

- **Model:** freemium
- **Pricing hypothesis:** Offer a premium tier that allows image uploads (via third‑party image host) and featured placement for $2/month.
- **Free tier:** All core features remain free, including unlimited listings and local storage favorites.
- **Free hosting:** GitHub Pages

## Potential operating costs

- Current prototype: ₹0 within published free‑tier limits

## Potential customers

- Local art collectives
- Community center craft programs
- Neighborhood hobby clubs

## Launch strategy

- Create a landing page on GitHub Pages and share in local art‑focused Facebook groups and community boards.
- Gather early feedback from a pilot neighborhood and iterate UI based on suggestions.
