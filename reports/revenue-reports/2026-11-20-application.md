# Revenue hypothesis — VolunteerMatch Local

- **Model:** freemium
- **Pricing hypothesis:** Offer a paid premium version for organizers that adds QR‑code generation for event check‑in and unlimited slot storage; free tier limited to 20 slots per month
- **Free tier:** All volunteer browsing and claiming features remain free; organizers can create up to 20 slots without payment
- **Free hosting:** GitHub Pages

## Potential operating costs

- Current prototype: ₹0 within published free‑tier limits

## Potential customers

- Neighborhood block parties
- Community garden clean‑ups
- Local school fundraisers
- Small NGOs running pop‑up clinics

## Launch strategy

- Publish the site on GitHub Pages and share the link in local community Facebook groups and neighborhood newsletters
- Gather feedback from 2‑3 small events and iterate UI based on volunteer experience

**Tagline:** Find a local way to help – instantly, no login required.
